## Phase C — OLS = per-L4 indicator search + single review gate

### Blueprint change
Replace the 5-task chain with 2:
- **2.5 `M` — OLS indicator search** (`a-ols-test`, format `olsTree`). No Y/X/param
  confirm screens. Runs the search, produces the tree.
- **2.5v `H` — Review & confirm selection** → decision `d-2.5` (reuse the existing
  choice-gate + freeze machinery). Per-L4 the reviewer can switch the chosen
  indicator (re-fit live) and expand advanced (Y / params override, default folded).
- **Delete `2.5y` / `2.5x` / `2.5p`** + their decisions. `heal_state` migrates saved
  projects (drop the dead tasks; re-derive). Keep `d-2.5` id/effects.

### Search algorithm (`ols_review.build_ols_search` or extend `build_ols_review`)
- **Y**: the single Metrics-Type Y from 2.1 (`model_selection(st).y`); no confirm.
- **Params**: `_propose_params(df)` (data-length driven, already added).
- **Candidates**: survivors of layers mapping→quality→signoff→stat
  (`drops_before(st, "selection")`), grouped by L4.
- **Coordinate-descent search**: seed each L4 with its highest |Pearson-vs-Y|
  candidate; fit; for every L4 whose Contribution/ROI is outside its knowledge-base
  range (`data_rules.match_factor_range`), try its other candidates, keep the one
  that lands in-range (or nearest). Objective: maximize #L4 in-range, tie-break by
  R². Fit budget capped (~30); record each trial to a search trace.
- **Advanced override:** reviewer can pin Y / change params in 2.5v; re-fit uses
  them. The chosen-per-L4 selection replaces the 2.5x tick-set as the ledger
  `selection` layer input; `d-2.5` freezes drops as today.
- Every fit is one traced `model.ols` invocation; batch the search trials under the
  2.5 task run.

### Contracts to keep in sync
`blueprint.py` ↔ `scenario.ts`; `models.py` ↔ `types.ts`; `ledger.model_selection`
reads the search result; `registry.py` wiring for 2.5/2.5v.
